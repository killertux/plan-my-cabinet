The downloaded file's SHA-256 is `e8aafa4f3656a108e8e91dd1681685455a4bf4f644cf01d4ecfa3fd80bf44df2`; PDF metadata identifies a May 2025 catalog, modified 2026-09-16. This hash identifies the inspected revision if the live URL changes. The review used a raster rendering of the actual page, not text extraction alone.

| Kit and exact plate | Kit `51MX153DRV00100*`, “Click 3D Slow Reta Calço 0 Compl.”; the asterisk says hinge + Calço 3D + body and cup covers. **Not verified / not supplied:** cup-screw hole diameter and pilot depth, plate-screw hole diameter and pilot depth, screw type/length, load rating, recommended hinge quantity, exact linkage path and safe clearances. The drawing shows 48 mm between cup fixing-hole centres, but this review does not authorize drilling those holes without the missing fastener details. Mark absent references “unavailable”; omit their numeric drilling instructions rather than inventing them.

**Rights decision:** the catalog page, product photography, line drawings, logos and QR code are **not bundled** or copied into the application or exported packets. The source is a research citation only; no permission to redistribute those assets was found in the inspected PDF.
